3 Complete Photo Guide — 28 Shots

Before You Shoot — Preparation (Do This First)

  1. Wash the car thoroughly — exterior, windows, wheels
  2. Vacuum interior completely — seats, boot, floor mats
  3. Wipe dashboard, door panels, centre console with a damp cloth
  4. Remove ALL personal items from the car (bags, ERP device cover, sunshades, everything)
  5. Apply tyre shine spray to all 4 tyres ($5 from any petrol kiosk or Sheng Siong)
  6. Clean the engine bay with a dry cloth — just remove dust and dirt

7 How to Contact Dealers — What to Say

What Information to Have Ready

  • Car plate number
  • Current mileage (check your odometer)
  • Last service date and mileage
  • Any accident history (be honest)
  • The new screen and rear camera upgrade
  • The fact that it is already Normal Plate (converted from ROPC)

Script — What to Say When You Call

"Hi, I'm a private owner looking to sell my car. It's a 2019 Mitsubishi Attrage 1.2 CVT, registered October 2020, plate number [YOUR PLATE]. COE expires October 2030 — about 4 years and 4 months remaining. It's already converted to Normal Plate, no restrictions. Current mileage is approximately [X] km. 3 previous owners. Recently upgraded infotainment screen and rear camera. I have an outstanding loan with Genie Financial Services. Could you give me a valuation? And can you tell me how you handle the settlement of the outstanding loan in the transaction?"

When They Give You a Price — How to Negotiate

  1. Get the offer in writing (WhatsApp message is fine)
  2. Call the next dealer and tell them: "I have an offer of $X from another dealer — can you beat it?"
  3. Keep doing this until you have 3–4 competing offers
  4. The highest offer is your real market price
  5. Go back to the second-highest and ask if they can match

Key Question to Ask Every Dealer

Always ask: "If your offer is lower than my outstanding loan, how does the shortfall work? Do you help arrange a payment plan with the finance company?"

Some dealers (especially consignment dealers) will help negotiate with Genie on your behalf. This is a useful service to look for.

About the Instagram Contact

8 How the Log Card & Loan Settlement Works

What is the Log Card?

The Vehicle Registration Certificate (commonly called the "log card") is the legal proof of vehicle ownership in Singapore. Genie Financial Services currently holds your log card because you have an outstanding hire-purchase loan. You cannot transfer ownership of the car without the log card being released.

The Sale Process Step by Step

1
Get your Early Settlement Figure from Genie

Call Genie: +65 6715 1518 | Website: genie.sg. Ask: "What is my early settlement amount as of today?" This is the exact amount needed to fully pay off the loan and get the log card released. This number may be different (likely lower) than the $66,000 total remaining payments figure.

2
Find a buyer and agree on price

Through your private listings or dealer contact. Agree on a price verbally first.

3
Collect a deposit from buyer

Typically 10% of the agreed price (e.g. $4,500 on a $45,000 sale). This secures the buyer's commitment. Get a signed Sales & Purchase Agreement (S&P). You can download a template from the LTA OneMotoring website.

4
Settle the Genie loan

When buyer pays the full balance, you use those funds to pay Genie the early settlement amount. If the sale price is less than the settlement amount, you must top up the difference before Genie releases the log card. Genie will process the release within 1–3 business days.

5
Genie releases the log card

Once the loan is fully settled, Genie releases the log card to you (or directly to the buyer if pre-arranged).

6
Transfer of ownership via LTA OneMotoring

Both buyer and seller log in to onemotoring.lta.gov.sg using SingPass. Seller initiates the transfer, buyer accepts. LTA transfer fee: $25. This can be done online — no need to go to LTA in person. Ownership transfers immediately upon completion.

If Your Sale Price Is Less Than the Settlement Amount

Example: If Genie's early settlement amount is $55,000 but you sell for $44,000, you must pay Genie $11,000 from your own funds before they release the log card. This is the "top-up" or shortfall amount. You can:

(a) Pay it in cash from savings
(b) Ask Genie to allow a payment arrangement for the shortfall
(c) Contact Credit Counselling Singapore (CCS) for free help negotiating with Genie — call 6225 5227
